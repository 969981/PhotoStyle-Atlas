# Split comparison mode

Split the 3:4 canvas nearly 1:1 with a clean straight boundary. Keep the upper source as real photography with restrained cinematic grading: warm natural light, slightly faded film color, soft contrast, faint haze, and fine grain. Preserve all source subjects, structures, perspective, material, and spatial relationships.

On warm ivory paper below, reinterpret the same scene with only the most recognizable contours and necessary environmental cues. Use refined simple shapes, sparse lines, imperfect strokes, and abundant continuous negative space. Preserve subject proportions, directions, and narrative relationships without fully tracing the background.

Keep small editorial text in an intact blank corner. Do not blend the drawing into the photograph.
