# Full-canvas mode

Remove the photographic region and translate the complete source scene into a minimal nostalgic editorial illustration. Preserve the original composition, subject count, pose, viewpoint, perspective, architecture or terrain, roads, occlusion, and narrative relationships.

Use warm ivory paper, matte simplified shapes, sparse handmade lines, subtle paper texture, and source-derived faded colors. Simplify repeated microdetail and photo noise, but keep every defining subject and structural feature. Negative space should arise naturally from sky, water, walls, road, or ground rather than deleting the main scene.

Add only a small source-grounded title or identifier in a low-information corner. The result should feel like an independent travel magazine and private notebook, not a cartoon or a regional pastiche.
