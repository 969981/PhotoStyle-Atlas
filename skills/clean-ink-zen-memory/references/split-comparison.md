# Split comparison mode

Split the 3:4 canvas nearly 1:1 with a straight clean boundary. Preserve the upper source as high-fidelity real photography. Keep subject identity, count, pose, structure, viewpoint, and light. Use restrained editorial grading with slightly reduced environmental saturation, deeper clean shadow masses, soft highlights, and nearly imperceptible grain; never create gray fog or dirty noise.

On pristine warm ivory paper below, reconstruct only the strongest visual anchor with pure black ink shapes and controlled stippling. The motif occupies about 35%–65% of the lower width and 20%–45% of its height. Preserve at least 60% continuous untouched paper.

Use solid ink for the core, sparse ink lines for defining structure, and crisp round dots only for intermediate values and edge recession. Do not let marks cross the boundary.
