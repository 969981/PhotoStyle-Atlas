# Full-canvas mode

Remove the photographic region and transform the complete 3:4 poster into a clean ink memory study on pristine warm ivory paper. Preserve the source subject's identity, count, pose, proportion, direction, defining structure, and key spatial relationships while reducing nonessential background detail.

The motif or scene fragment occupies about 25%–55% of the canvas, centered or offset according to source balance. Preserve at least 60% continuous untouched paper. Use pure black solid ink for the main weight, a few economical lines, and crisp controlled dots that become fewer toward the edge.

All unmarked paper must remain bright, even, and clean. The result should feel contemplative and editorial, never dusty, charcoal-like, stained, or digitally pixelated.
