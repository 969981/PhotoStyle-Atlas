# Minimal-zine material presets

Presets define paper, reproduction, type, and color character. Composition mode and subject route remain independent. `minimal-zine-standard` is the adaptive default.

## `minimal-zine-standard` — adaptive default

- Paper: warm ivory or gray-beige scanned uncoated stock with restrained fibres and tonal drift
- Process: choose one of soft photocopy, newsprint halftone, restrained risograph, or faded film print according to the subject
- Type: small old-book serif plus typewriter/monospaced metadata; text may be absent
- Ink: derive one hue from the subject; cobalt `#1646B8` is the fallback
- Geometry: select from the seven composition modes; do not force the `airy-fragment` proportions onto every image
- Mood: quiet, authored, tactile, observant, slightly distant
- Best for: landscapes, portraits, objects, architecture, concepts, photo restyles, social series, and evidence fragments
- Avoid: generic beige poster, meaningless microtext, giant campaign title, decorative pseudo-archive codes, uniform vintage filter

## `weathered-horizon`

- Paper: pale cool-gray or salt-worn ivory stock
- Process: faded film print or cyanotype plate with one dissolved edge
- Type: one tiny place/weather line only when real; optional short serif phrase
- Ink: cyanotype blue `#0B4F7A`, ultramarine, or vermilion for a warm horizon event
- Modes: `panorama-strip`, `photo-window`, `dual-frame`
- Mood: coast, rain, distance, weather, travel memory
- Best for: landscape, street, sea, skyline, train-window, and weather images
- Avoid: postcard saturation, generic birds, fake weather metadata, cinematic teal-orange grading

## `portrait-carbon`

- Paper: soft bone or cool copier stock
- Process: carbon-rich photocopy, fine newsprint halftone, or one-color photo plate
- Type: a small name or phrase beside the face; no text across eyes or mouth
- Ink: cobalt, tomato red, or magenta as a crop, garment plate, or adjacent paper field
- Modes: `portrait-archive`, `photo-window`, `dual-frame`
- Mood: intimate, documentary, musical, literary
- Best for: people, self-portraits, profiles, authors, musicians, and memory portraits
- Avoid: face regeneration, cosmetic color marks, fashion-ad retouching, invented identity details

## `specimen-index`

- Paper: cooler gray-white stock with visible fibres and a faint registration edge
- Process: soft halftone or object photocopy with one flat riso plate
- Type: one real object name, material, or small measurement; optional index number only when supplied
- Ink: lemon yellow, pear green, cobalt, or tomato red
- Modes: `specimen-plate`, `airy-fragment`, `dual-frame`
- Mood: field note, observation, collection, material study
- Best for: plants, food, ceramics, tools, books, garments, products, and found objects
- Avoid: glossy pedestal, price badge, logo lockup, invented catalogue code, scrapbook labels

## `architectural-trace`

- Paper: pale stone-gray or warm drafting stock
- Process: faded photo print with photocopied line trace or restrained riso plate
- Type: one real place line or short phrase aligned to an opening, stair, or shadow
- Ink: cobalt, cyanotype blue, tomato red, or lemon yellow derived from the site
- Modes: `photo-window`, `panorama-strip`, `dual-frame`
- Mood: spatial, measured, quiet, inhabited
- Best for: buildings, interiors, windows, stairs, streets, and structural details
- Avoid: impossible perspective, generic brutalism, real-estate copy, decorative blueprint overlays

## `blue-window`

- Paper: warm bone scanned paper
- Process: restrained risograph grain and a small plate offset
- Image: grayscale photo touching an opaque cobalt window or partial-color region
- Type: short serif phrase at the image edge; real metadata only
- Modes: `airy-fragment`, `photo-window`, `portrait-archive`
- Mood: night, memory, solitude
- Avoid: a large clean digital rectangle with no printed integration

## `yellow-specimen`

- Paper: cool gray-white stock
- Process: soft halftone photo plus slightly rough yellow riso plate
- Image: stair, tool, chair, plant, garment, food, or found object
- Type: drifting typewriter letters and one tiny real caption
- Modes: `specimen-plate`, `dual-frame`
- Mood: observation, afternoon, found moment
- Avoid: cheerful commercial yellow, stickers, scrapbook collage

## `red-xerox-fragment`

- Paper: cool off-white copier stock with a faint fold
- Process: toner bloom, clipped blacks, and one repeat ghost
- Image: torn black photocopy or document crop with tomato-red interruption
- Type: restrained typewriter metadata plus one rough serif phrase
- Modes: `airy-fragment`, `dual-frame`, `type-object`
- Mood: friction, warning, candid edit
- Avoid: horror dirt, warning-sign graphics, oversized red advertising type

## `moon-dual-panel`

- Paper: pale cool-gray aged sheet
- Process: faded film grain and soft newsprint dots
- Image: two adjacent or overlapping photos with a narrow gap
- Type: one short line crossing the relation and an optional real footer
- Ink: ultramarine, cyanotype blue, or a subject-derived seam color
- Modes: `dual-frame`, `panorama-strip`
- Mood: comparison, distance, quiet relation
- Avoid: symmetrical corporate columns and fully labelled comparisons

## `typewriter-map`

- Paper: gray-beige archival stock
- Process: letterpress bleed with photocopy-soft map, proof, route, or contour material
- Image: real map/document texture or one concept fragment
- Type: irregular serif/typewriter hierarchy; one rough printed word may lead
- Ink: cobalt, magenta, or pear green
- Modes: `type-object`, `airy-fragment`
- Mood: searching, choosing, slightly surreal
- Avoid: invented coordinates, full-page Swiss typography, fake technical authority

## Selection guide

| Subject or intention | First choice | Second choice |
|---|---|---|
| general or mixed subject | `minimal-zine-standard` | `blue-window` |
| landscape, travel, weather | `weathered-horizon` | `blue-window` |
| portrait, profile, memory of a person | `portrait-carbon` | `minimal-zine-standard` |
| object, product, food, botanical | `specimen-index` | `yellow-specimen` |
| building, interior, street | `architectural-trace` | `weathered-horizon` |
| warning, friction, strong edit | `red-xerox-fragment` | `typewriter-map` |
| two related views or comparison | `moon-dual-panel` | `minimal-zine-standard` |
| conceptual route or text-led idea | `typewriter-map` | `blue-window` |

## Variation rule

Lock paper, lead process, type pairing, and hue for a coherent series. Vary composition mode, crop, image scale, cluster position, and color form. Do not create variety by adding unrelated decorations or switching material process on every card.
