# Full-canvas mode

Remove the photographic region and turn the complete 3:4 poster into a restrained charcoal memory study. Preserve the source subject's identity, count, pose, proportions, position, direction, viewpoint, perspective, and defining structure.

Select 1–2 strongest anchors and place them at roughly 18%–35% of the canvas, centered, lower-centered, or offset according to source balance. Include no more than 1–3 minimal environmental traces. Let all other content return to untouched warm white paper.

Keep charcoal dust, crumbs, rubbing, and eraser marks localized to the subject and its fading edge. The rest of the page must stay bright, clean, and free of global gray haze.
