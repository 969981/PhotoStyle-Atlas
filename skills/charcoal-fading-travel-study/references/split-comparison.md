# Split comparison mode

Split the 3:4 canvas nearly 1:1 with a clean deliberate boundary. Keep the upper source as a clearly real photograph with exact subject identity, count, pose, proportions, direction, perspective, material, natural light, and original color atmosphere. Apply only subtle editorial grading and very fine film grain.

On warm white drawing paper below, select 1–2 strongest memory anchors rather than redrawing the whole scene. The charcoal subject occupies about 18%–35% of the lower area. Add no more than 1–3 quiet environmental traces, such as a broken horizon, a few terrain marks, a cable line, tree marks, or a small contact shadow.

Keep the remaining lower paper untouched and clean. Do not blend charcoal into the photo.
