# Full-canvas mode

Remove the source-photo comparison region. Keep the entire 3:4 environment as real photography with only subtle editorial grading and fine film grain. Adapt through natural cropping or modest environmental extension without moving buildings or distorting subjects.

Convert the main 1–4 narrative people into warm-white paper sketch figures. Every other recognizable person still visible must also become a simpler paper cutout at the true count; do not leave a mixture of photographic and paper people. Preserve exact pose, position, scale, interaction, hand contact, occlusion, and ground contact.

Retain 20%–32% low-information space for breathing and text. The final image must read as a real photographed place inhabited by physically credible thin paper figures.
