# Split comparison mode

Split the 3:4 canvas nearly 1:1 with a straight clean boundary. The upper section remains a high-fidelity real photograph with only subtle editorial grading and fine film grain. Preserve landmark structure, perspective, materials, environment, and light; do not illustrate, move, replace, mirror, stretch, or distort the source.

In the lower section, place the folk ink portrait on warm-white matte paper. It occupies about 36%–55% of the lower area, with 45%–60% continuous paper white. Keep only the 2–4 most defining structures and a minimal amount of environment. Place the text in a separate blank zone opposite the landmark's visual offset.

Do not blend the two sections or let ink and color enter the photo.
