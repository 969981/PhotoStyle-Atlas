# Full-canvas mode

Remove the photographic region and transform the complete 3:4 poster into the folk ink-and-color portrait. Preserve the source landmark's identity, viewpoint, perspective, proportions, defining geometry, and orientation while simplifying nonessential detail.

Place the landmark on warm-white matte paper at roughly 36%–55% of the canvas, centered, lower-centered, or offset according to source balance. Preserve 45%–60% continuous blank paper and at least one untouched area with no image or text. Do not compress the entire photographed background into the poster.

Separate the text and landmark into distinct paper-white zones with a visible breathing gap. Keep the medium flat, handmade, and editorial rather than photographic or digitally polished.
