# Full-canvas mode

Remove the photographic region and transform the complete 3:4 poster into one two-color kraft stencil print. Preserve landmark identity, count, orientation, overall proportions, viewpoint, top, base, and 2–6 defining internal openings.

Use one continuous dark natural-fiber paper field and one opaque light stencil ink. The landmark occupies about 55%–85% of the canvas height and 30%–70% of its width, centered or offset according to source balance. Keep at most one minimal ground, shoreline, rock, or water connection and no complete background.

Keep the upper silhouette firm while gradually reducing ink coverage toward the lower edge through irregular stencil gaps and exposed paper fibers. Place exactly one truthful uppercase English scene word in edge space at about 10%–22% of canvas width. Render no other text.
