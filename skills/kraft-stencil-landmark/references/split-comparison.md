# Split comparison mode

Split the 3:4 canvas nearly 1:1 with a straight clean boundary. Keep the upper region as a substantial high-fidelity source photograph with only subtle travel or architecture editorial grading and fine film grain. Preserve landmark identity, count, orientation, proportions, viewpoint, people, sky, ground, water, materials, light, and color atmosphere.

Use the lower region as one continuous dark natural-fiber paper field. Print the same landmark as one opaque light negative stencil shape occupying about 55%–85% of the lower height and 28%–68% of its width. Keep only 2–6 defining internal dark openings and at most one minimal ground, shoreline, rock, or water connection.

Place the required single English scene word in lower edge space at about 10%–22% of the lower width. Do not let print or type cross into the photograph.
