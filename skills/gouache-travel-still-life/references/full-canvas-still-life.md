# Full-canvas still-life mode

Remove the photographic region. From the source, select 4–8 real objects or visual fragments that carry the subject, place, and memory. Recompose them as one asymmetrical travel-object archive; scale, overlap, and placement may change, but identity and defining features must remain.

The still-life cluster occupies roughly 45%–68% of the 3:4 canvas, centered, lower-centered, or offset according to source direction. Preserve one large continuous warm off-white blank zone. Do not line objects evenly along the bottom, fill the background, or add decorative filler.

Compress the source into 4–7 principal colors. Lock a 1–3-word uppercase English title, STUDY NO. XX, and a 5–10-word observation, then paint the title in the same handmade gouache language.
