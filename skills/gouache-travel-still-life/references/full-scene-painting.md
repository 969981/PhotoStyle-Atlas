# Full-scene painting mode

Remove the photographic region and repaint the complete source scene in opaque gouache. Preserve the original composition, subject count, pose, position, direction, architecture or terrain, roads, object relationships, occlusion, viewpoint, perspective, and light. Do not extract objects, rearrange them as a still life, replace the background, or invent content.

Simplify only repetitive windows, foliage, masonry, distant crowds, tiny signage, and photographic noise while retaining every identity-defining structure. Adapt to 3:4 through natural cropping or modest extension of sky, ground, or surrounding environment; never stretch or distort.

Compress the source into 6–10 clear color steps. Use broad matte blocks, loose dark outlines, visible bristles, controlled dry gaps, exposed paper, and direct white highlights. Small editorial text is optional and must stay in a genuine low-information area.
