# Split still-life mode

Split the 3:4 canvas nearly 1:1 with a clean straight boundary. Preserve the upper source photograph with exact subject identity, count, pose, proportions, direction, viewpoint, materials, natural light, and color atmosphere. Apply only subtle travel-editorial grading and very fine film grain.

For the lower section, select 4–8 real objects or visual fragments that best represent the subject, place, and travel memory. Recompose them as an asymmetrical still life while preserving identity and defining features. The cluster occupies about 45%–68% of the lower area and sits within continuous warm off-white paper.

Lock a 1–3-word uppercase English title, STUDY NO. XX, and a 5–10-word observation. Keep them readable in the blank area. Do not blend paint into the photograph.
